> **This is an example post-mortem** showing the format. Delete it before you publish real ideas.

## Recap

I put on a DV01-neutral 2s10s steepener at +45bp, targeting +75bp with a stop at +30bp. The stop was hit on 20 August for a loss of **−1.0R**.

## What happened

Two weak data prints in July triggered a growth scare. The long end rallied hard while the front end barely moved, so the curve bull-flattened through my stop. Auctions were well absorbed, so the supply story never got its chance.

## Thesis right? Trade right?

| | Verdict | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Thesis | Untested | Term premium never became the driver; growth did |
| Expression | Reasonable | DV01-neutral kept me out of the parallel move |
| Timing | Poor | Entered before evidence of weak auction demand |
| Risk management | Good | Stop honoured, loss limited to 1R |

## What I would do differently

Wait for confirmation: one or two auctions with weak demand at the tails before entering. I could also have expressed the view in a way that survives a growth scare, for example a steepener in the 5s30s sector, or a payer swaption on 10y that loses only the premium.

## Lessons

1. A supply thesis needs a **catalyst calendar**, not just a narrative.
2. Ask *which macro regime breaks this trade* before entry, and check how likely that regime is.
3. Stops work. Losing 1R on a wrong regime is the system working as designed.
