Hi! I'm Sean 👋🏻 I study Business Administration and Data Science at UC Berkeley and have experience at Ares Management, BACO Properties, and the Center for Effective Global Action. This site is where I'm learning macro trading out loud.

I'm endlessly curious about markets, from public equities to alternative investments, and right now I'm obsessed with macro, where one jobs report or central bank press conference can send ripples through rates, currencies, and stocks in a single afternoon?! Here, I turn that curiosity into research and trade ideas, write down my thinking before the outcome, and revisit what I got right and wrong.

Off-screen, I'm usually chasing a ball (basketball, tennis, soccer, or golf), planning my next trip, or happily disappearing down a Wikipedia or YouTube rabbit hole.

## What you'll find here

**Trade ideas:** Each one is written and published before the outcome is known, with an entry, stop, target, size in units of risk (R), and the conditions that would prove me wrong.

**Post-mortems:** Every closed idea gets an honest review, win or lose. I ask was the thesis right, and was the trade right?

## How I think about a trade

1. **What's already priced?** Read the market's expectations from futures, curves, forwards, options, etc.
2. **Where do I differ, and why?** A view matters if it isn't already in the price.
3. **What's the cleanest expression?** Choose the instrument that pays the most for being right about this view, and the least for being wrong about something else.
4. **What breaks it?** Write the exit down before entering, and respect it.

## Track record

Every idea, open and closed, lives on the [Trade Ideas](/trades/) page with its result in R. Losers stay up next to winners.
