## What you'll find here

**Trade ideas.** Each one is published before the outcome is known, with an explicit entry, stop, target, sizing in units of risk (R), and the conditions that would prove me wrong.

**Post-mortems.** Every closed idea gets a review, win or lose. I ask whether the thesis was right and whether the trade was right, which are often two different questions.

## How I think about a trade

1. **What is priced?** Read the market's expectations from futures, curves, forwards and implied volatility.
2. **Where do I differ, and why?** A view only matters if it differs from what's priced.
3. **What is the best expression?** Choose the instrument with the best payoff for *this* view, not the most obvious one.
4. **What breaks it?** Write down the invalidation before entry, and respect it.

## Track record

All ideas, open and closed, are listed on the [Trade Ideas](/trades/) page with results in R. The site's full history is public on GitHub, so anyone can check when each idea was first published.
